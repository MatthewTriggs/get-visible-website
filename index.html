FAQ

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. We build a working demo of your website within 48 hours, at no cost and with no commitment. If it's not right for you, you walk away.

How does payment work?

Once you're happy with the demo and want to go ahead, we ask for a 50% deposit to start the final build. The other 50% is due when your site is finished, before it goes live. Payment is by EFT, and all prices are in rand, including VAT where applicable.

How long until my website is live?

Your demo is ready within 48 hours, and most sites go live within a week. The main thing that speeds it up is getting your content to us quickly.

What do I need to give you?

Your text, photos, logo and business details. We ask for these within 5 business days of starting, so we can keep to the timeline. Not sure what to write? Send us what you have and we'll help shape it.

Can I ask for changes?

Of course. Every project includes two rounds of revisions. Extra rounds are R350 per hour, and we'll always tell you before any billable work starts. Bigger changes, like new pages or features, are quoted separately.

Do I own my website?

Your text, photos and logo are always yours. Once you've paid in full, you have full rights to use your website. The underlying code and templates stay ours, and we license them to you for as long as you're a client in good standing. The details are in our Terms of Service.

How do the care plans work?

Care plans cover hosting, security updates and backups after launch, with content updates on the higher tiers. There's a 3-month minimum, then it runs month to month. To cancel, email info@getvisible.co.za with 30 days' notice.

How do I order content creatives?

Send your product photos on WhatsApp and tell us where you'll post them. Creatives start at R250 each, arrive within 24 to 48 hours, and there's no monthly contract.
