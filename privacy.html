Privacy Policy

How Get Visible collects, uses, and protects your personal information, in plain English and in compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who We Are

Get Visible is a South African web design business based in Pretoriuspark, Pretoria, operating at getvisible.co.za. We build affordable, professional websites for South African small businesses.

Get Visible is registered with the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission (CIPC), registration number .

Our Information Officer, responsible for POPIA compliance, is Matthew Triggs. For any privacy-related queries or to exercise your rights below, contact us at info@getvisible.co.za.

This policy was last updated in June 2026.

What Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when making an enquiry or requesting a quote. This includes:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Business name

We do not collect any sensitive personal information (such as ID numbers, financial details, or health information), and we do not collect information from children.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry or quote request
  • Discuss and deliver web design services you have requested
  • Send you relevant information about your project (e.g. invoices, progress updates)

We will not use your information to send unsolicited marketing communications without your consent.

Cookies and Analytics

We currently use no tracking cookies and no analytics tools on this website. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any similar third-party tracking services.

If this changes in future, this policy will be updated and a cookie consent mechanism will be added.

Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. Ever.

This website is hosted and delivered via Cloudflare. As your requests pass through Cloudflare's network, they may process certain technical data (such as IP addresses) in the course of providing their infrastructure and security services. Cloudflare acts as a data processor on our behalf. You can review Cloudflare's privacy policy at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

This website also loads fonts from Google Fonts. As a result, your device's IP address and browser information is sent to Google when a page loads. This is a technical requirement of displaying the fonts and is not used by us for tracking. You can review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

No other third parties have access to your personal information.

How We Store and Protect Your Data

Enquiries submitted via WhatsApp or email are stored only within those communication platforms (WhatsApp and our email provider). We do not maintain a separate database of customer personal information.

We take reasonable precautions to ensure that any information shared with us is handled securely and accessed only by people who need it to deliver our services.

Your Rights Under POPIA

The Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) gives you the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • The right to know what personal information we hold about you
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate information
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information
  • The right to object to the processing of your information
  • The right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, including requesting deletion of your data, email us at info@getvisible.co.za. We will respond within a reasonable time and at no charge to you.

Information Regulator

If you believe your personal information has been mishandled, you have the right to lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator:

  • Website: inforegulator.org.za
  • Email: inforeg@justice.gov.za

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

For any questions about this policy, contact us at info@getvisible.co.za.
